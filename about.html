About Jisr El-Khair

Connecting generous people with families in need across Algeria.

Who We Are

Jisr El-Khair is a charitable platform designed to connect people who wish to donate items with families who truly need them. Our goal is to make donating simple, safe and accessible throughout Algeria.

❤️

Our Mission

Encourage people to give unused items a second life and help families in need.

🌍

Our Vision

Build a stronger community where generosity becomes part of everyday life.

🤝

Our Values

Trust, respect and kindness guide everything we do.
